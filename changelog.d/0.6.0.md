### Added

- Nine generations in one run. `state.json` gains `seat`, the names on the stone,
  and `day`, the day inside a generation; a rule counts a day per action once the
  gate is open and a year after every twelfth.
- The rollover: when the third year ends the run seats a name — `seat` gains one,
  the year and the day start over, the wall gives up three courses, the field
  gives up two, the granary takes in ten stone, and the three disciples come back
  whole. Nine generations bring the stone to nine names.
- The waves now read the seat as well as the year: the first three generations
  ask for a wall of three, six and nine; the next three ask four, seven and nine;
  the last three ask five, eight and nine.
