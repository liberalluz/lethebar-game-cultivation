### Added

- A clock: the run now counts thirty-six days, and the year turns at day twelve,
  twenty-four and thirty-six. `manifest.json` declares `clock.per: turn` and the
  `clock@1` capability, and the year rules read the engine's `tick`.
- The economy: an herb field pays one, two or three stone a day as it grows, and
  each year adds one, two or three of upkeep, so a run that never dispatches
  cannot feed itself.
- Two ways to spend stone: `dig-wall` raises the wall by one for four stone, and
  `tend-field` breaks one more acre for three. Both are gated on the stone they
  cost, because the engine pays `costs` without checking them.
- Three waves. Each year mark asks a wall of three, six and nine; a wall short of
  the mark sets the year's `breach`, and the phase machine ends the run with that
  year's reason.

### Changed

- `phases.json` now runs thirty-six turns with three year ranks, and its failure
  list carries the granary and the three waves beside the three old wounds.
- `stone` keeps its old labels and gains a fourth at thirty; its ceiling rises
  from twenty to sixty so a good year has somewhere to go.

### Fixed

- The English table gains the labels for every new resource value and the four
  new failure reasons, so the locale gap check stays clean without touching the
  first act's nineteen strings.
